Strength Calculation

As part of my degree in Production Technology i have learned to calculate how much a construction can withhold. We did this first in hand and later as a FEA (Finite Element Analysis) in SolidWorks. By using Granta EduPack it was possible to set the needed boundaries and pick a handful of materials and then deciding on one material by using the Ashby’s method.
