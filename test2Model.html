01 — Production

Production
Technology

From idea to manufacturable object.

Production technology connects design, engineering and physical manufacturing. The process is about understanding how an object is constructed, how its individual parts work together, and how an idea can become a precise and repeatable product.

Digital modelling makes it possible to explore proportions, assemblies, materials and production methods before the physical object is created.
