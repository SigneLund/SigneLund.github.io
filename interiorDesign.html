Interior Design

Visual Presentations:

As part of my degree as an interior design consultant we were firstly taught to draw by hand. The drawings were floor plans, front perspectives, perspective drawings, isometric drawings, elevations and section drawings.. We learned how to draw them perfectly to scale. After the second half of the course we were allowed to use AutoCAD. As there were no help from the school on how to use it I taught myself by watching Youtube videos and i used it to finish my degree.

What is it?

School of Interior Design Copenhagen is a private institution, focused entirely on interior design. The education is organized as e-learning (self-study) and there is no compulsory attendance or instruction at the school. The school's curriculum is arranged in cooperation with different professionals, who all have knowledge and experience within their respective areas of work.
